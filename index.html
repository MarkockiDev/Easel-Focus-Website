A focus timer for iPhone & iPad

A quiet moment
with a painting.

Choose how long you’ll focus. A masterpiece from The Met slowly emerges from the fog while you work, and at 0:00 it’s fully unveiled, and yours to keep.

Free, with no ads. For iPhone and iPad.

An exhibition guide

Seven rooms, one quiet idea

No points, no pets, no noise. Just a painting, slowly coming into view, as long as you stay with your work.

I The reveal

Focus, and the fog lifts

Every session hangs a new painting behind a soft, drifting fog. It clears from the centre outward as the minutes pass, so you can feel your progress without watching the clock.

  • Fully unveiled at 0:00, with a museum placard: what you focused on, the title, the artist and the date.
  • Never waits on the network. The painting is ready before you begin.
  • Leave and come back. The timer keeps true time, even with your phone locked.

II Your gallery

Every painting you uncover is yours to keep

Finished sessions hang in your own gallery. Over weeks it becomes a quiet record of everything you’ve made time for.

  • Over 600 works from The Met’s Open Access collection, from Vermeer and Van Gogh to Hokusai.
  • Focus with a collection: European Paintings, Japanese Prints, Flowers & Birds, American Paintings, or a single artist or era.
  • Share a painting in a gilded frame, as a Story or a Post.

III Make it yours

What will you focus on?

Name what you’re working on, or pick a subject: study, writing, reading, creating. Then choose a single session or a Pomodoro set, five minutes to two hours.

  • Pomodoro blocks with short breaks, a new painting for each block.
  • Your intention travels with the painting into your gallery.
  • Start from anywhere: Siri, Shortcuts, Control Center or a widget.

IV Your focus, in numbers

Streaks, statistics and a Year in Review

See your hours, your paintings and your longest run, a week at a glance and a calendar of every day you showed up.

  • Milestones at 10, 25, 50, 100 paintings and beyond.
  • Year in Review, every December: your year in focus as a short exhibition, set to Handel.
  • Private by design. Your history and intentions stay on your device.

V Beyond the app

On your Lock Screen, Home Screen and Dynamic Island

The Painting of the Day widget starts each morning hidden in fog. Every minute you focus clears a little more, until your daily goal unveils it completely.

  • Live Activity keeps your countdown on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.
  • Focus & Streak widget, StandBy, and a Control Center button.
  • A gentle daily reminder that names today’s painting, if you’d like one.

VI By day, by night

A gallery that keeps your hours

A warm, lit wall by day, and a candlelit charcoal room for late sessions. Choose one, or let Easel follow your iPhone.

VII Patronage

Every gallery has its patrons

Easel is free, with no ads and no subscription, and made by one person. If it brings some calm to your days, a one-time gift helps keep it going. It unlocks nothing, because everything in Easel is already yours.
