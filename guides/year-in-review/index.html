IX  The year

Year in Review: your year in focus

Every December, Easel looks back on your year as a short, self-playing exhibition, set to Handel’s Water Music, ending with a card you can share.

When it appears

From 15 December to 2 January each year, an invitation appears at the top of Statistics: “Your year in Easel”, with Enter the exhibition. In January it looks back on the year that just ended. Sessions up to 31 December are always included.

If notifications are on, Easel sends at most three gentle reminders in that season: on 15 December, 30 December and 2 January.

What’s inside

  1. The year.
  2. Your hours in focus, and when you focus best (morning, afternoon or evening).
  3. Paintings revealed, and the artist you kept most.
  4. Your longest run of days, and your fullest month.
  5. The painting of the year: the one you earned with your longest single focus.
  6. A thank-you, and a gilded card of your year to share as a Story or post.

Watching it

  • It plays by itself, a few seconds per page.
  • Tap the right side to skip ahead, the left side to go back.
  • Touch and hold to pause.
  • Swipe down to close.
  • With VoiceOver, swipe to move between pages.
