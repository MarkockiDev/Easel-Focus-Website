IV  Pomodoro

Using Easel as a Pomodoro timer

Pomodoro breaks long work into focused blocks with short rests between them. In Easel, every block reveals its own painting, so a full set leaves four new works in your gallery.

How a Pomodoro set works

  • Four focus blocks of 25 minutes each.
  • A 5-minute break after each of the first three blocks.
  • A new painting in every block, each revealed from fog like a single session.

Starting a set

  1. On the home screen, tap Pomodoro.
  2. Choose a collection under Focus with, if you like.
  3. Tap Begin. The focus question shows the set as 4 × 25 min.

During a break

When a block ends, its painting is revealed and saved, and your break begins. The break screen shows the painting you just earned and a countdown. Rest, stretch, look at the painting. When you’re ready, tap Begin next focus; you can start before the break is over.

Ending a set early

Tap End during a block or a break. Blocks you’ve already finished stay in your gallery; the block in progress doesn’t count.

Tip

Pomodoro completions are celebrated with their own haptic pattern when the fourth painting is revealed. Turn haptics on or off in Settings → During a session.
