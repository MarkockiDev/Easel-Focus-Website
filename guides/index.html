The exhibition guide

How to use Easel

Short, illustrated guides to every room of the app, from your first session to widgets, Pomodoro and Year in Review. If you ever lose your way in Easel, you’ll find it again here.

I

Getting started

Your first focus session in Easel, step by step: choose how long to focus, begin, and watch a painting from The Met emerge from the fog until it’s fully revealed at 0:00.

II

Focus sessions

How a focus session works in Easel: the fog reveal, the timer, leaving the app or locking your phone, ending early, ambient music, sound effects and haptics.

III

What will you focus on?

Give each Easel session a purpose: type an intention, reuse a recent one, or choose a subject like Study, Work, Writing or Reading.

IV

Pomodoro

Easel’s Pomodoro mode: four 25-minute focus blocks with 5-minute breaks, a new painting revealed in every block.

V

Collections

Focus with all 606 paintings or choose a collection in Easel: European Paintings, Japanese Prints, American Paintings, Flowers & Birds, a single artist like Hokusai or Rembrandt, or an era.

VI

Your gallery

Every painting you reveal in Easel hangs in your gallery.

VII

Sharing

Share any painting you earned in Easel as a framed card, in Story (9:16) or Post (4:5) format.

VIII

Statistics & streaks

How Easel counts your focus time, paintings and streaks: the week chart, the 16-week focus calendar, your current and best streak, and milestones at 10, 25, 50 and 100 paintings.

IX

Year in Review

Every December, Easel’s Year in Review turns your year of focus into a short exhibition: hours in focus, paintings revealed, your favourite artist, longest streak and the painting of the year.

X

Widgets & Live Activities

Add Easel’s Painting of the Day and Focus & Streak widgets to your Home Screen, Lock Screen or StandBy, follow sessions in a Live Activity, and start focusing from Siri, Shortcuts or Control Center.

XI

Reminders

Turn on Easel’s gentle daily reminder, choose its time, and learn which other notifications Easel can send and how to turn each one off.

XII

Settings & appearance

A tour of Easel’s Settings: By day, By night or Automatic appearance, ambient music, sound effects and haptics, daily goal, language, the introduction, privacy and feedback.

XIII

Patronage

Easel is free, with no ads and no subscription.

XIV

Troubleshooting

Help for Easel: getting back to the home screen, a painting that won’t load, a missing Live Activity or reminder, the widget fog not clearing, keyboard shortcuts on iPad, and how to contact us.
