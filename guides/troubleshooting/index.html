Getting back to the home screen

  • Gallery and Settings close with ‹ at the top left (or a swipe from the edge). The gallery slides back to the left, Settings to the right.
  • Statistics closes with ⌄ at the top left.
  • Sheets (collections, share, museum label) close with Done or a swipe down.
  • The focus question closes with ‹ without starting a session.
  • During a session, End (then confirm) returns you home.
  • On iPad with a keyboard, Esc goes back from anywhere.

“The painting couldn’t be loaded”

Easel downloads each new painting before the session starts, so it needs an internet connection when you tap Begin. Check your connection and tap Try Again. Paintings you’ve already seen are kept on your device, so your gallery still works offline.

No Live Activity on the Lock Screen

Open the iPhone Settings app → Apps → Easel and turn on Live Activities. If you tapped Don’t Allow on the first prompt, this is where to change it.

The daily reminder doesn’t arrive

  • Check that notifications are on in the iPhone Settings app → Apps → Easel → Notifications.
  • If you’ve already focused today, the reminder waits until tomorrow. That’s by design.
  • Focus modes and Scheduled Summary can hold notifications back.

The widget’s fog isn’t clearing

Only completed sessions clear the fog; sessions ended early don’t. Your daily goal is set in Settings → Focus each day. Widgets refresh when Easel saves a session; if one seems stuck, open Easel once.

My streak reset

A streak counts consecutive days with at least one completed session. A day with no completed session ends it. Your best streak is kept in Statistics.

Moving to a new iPhone

Your gallery and history live on your device. They move with an iPhone backup or a direct transfer to a new iPhone; Easel has no account or server of its own. Deleting Easel deletes them.
