XIII  Support

Patronage: supporting Easel

Easel is free and made by one person. Every gallery has its patrons; if Easel brings some calm to your days, you can become one of them. Only if you’d like to.

The gifts

Open Settings → Support → Become a Patron. There are three one-time gifts:

  • A Fine Brush: for the small, careful strokes that make the app better.
  • A Box of Pigments: fresh colour for whatever comes next.
  • A Gilded Frame: hangs the whole work on the wall. A true patron’s gift.

Prices are shown in your local currency by the App Store.

What a tip does

Nothing changes in the app: everything in Easel is already yours. Tips are one-time purchases, never subscriptions, and are handled entirely by Apple. Once you’ve tipped, the Saturday note stops.

Other ways to help that cost nothing: rate Easel on the App Store, share a painting, or send an idea on the feedback board.
